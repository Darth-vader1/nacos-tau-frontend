Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run the NACOS TAU portal, sell event and game tickets, and keep the site secure. We never sell your data. Card details are entered on our payment provider's page and never reach us. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at nacos@tau.edu.ng.

1. Who we are

This portal (nacosportal.vercel.app) is run by the Nigeria Association of Computing Students (NACOS), Thomas Adewumi University Chapter, University Drive, Off Idofin Road, Oko-Irese, Kwara State, Nigeria. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: nacos@tau.edu.ng, +234 812 639 8496.

2. What we collect

WhoWhat we collect
Students (members)Name, school email, matric number, department and level; anything you choose to add to your profile (photo, phone number, social links, skills, interests); and your directory visibility settings.
Guests (non-members buying tickets)Name, email address and phone number, entered at checkout.
Everyone who paysWhat you bought (event, number of tickets, games and plays), the amount, fees, payment reference, ticket number and time. We do not see or store card or bank details: you enter those on the payment provider's checkout page.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: your account, event registration, tickets, receipts, game plays and hackathon entries.
  • To send you emails about it: ticket confirmations, receipts, game marshal invitations, and NACOS announcements for members.
  • To keep the portal secure: stopping fraud, spam and repeated failed logins, and investigating problems.
  • To keep proper records: payments and fees, as any organisation handling money must.
  • With your choice: optional profile details and whether other students can find you in the directory. You can change these at any time in your dashboard.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. We share it only with the services that run the portal for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase: database and login, hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Vercel: hosts the website and server.
  • Bachs: processes payments and handles your card details on its own checkout page.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers the emails we send.
  • Upstash: temporary request counters used to block abuse.

Within NACOS, other students see only what you choose to show in the student directory; everything else about members and buyers is visible only to portal admins. Game marshals see only a player's name and their games and plays, never contact or payment details. We may also share data where the law or a university disciplinary process requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Accounts: while you are a member. Ask us and we will close your account and delete your profile.
  • Payment and ticket records: as long as needed for NACOS's financial records and to resolve any dispute or refund.
  • Unfinished checkouts: marked "abandoned" after 24 hours; they are kept only as a record that the checkout was started.
  • Security logs and play logs: as long as needed to keep the portal secure and to settle questions about an event.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong (most profile details you can edit yourself);
  • delete your data, unless we must keep a record (for example, a payment);
  • stop or limit how we use it, or withdraw a choice you made (such as directory visibility).

Email nacos@tau.edu.ng from the address you used with us. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Browser storage

The portal stores your login session and a few settings (such as a checkout in progress) in your browser so it works properly. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

8. Keeping it safe

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so the public site can only read what is meant to be public, and sensitive actions are logged. No system is perfect: if we ever learn of a breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.

9. Changes

We'll update this page when how we use data changes, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced on the portal or by email.
